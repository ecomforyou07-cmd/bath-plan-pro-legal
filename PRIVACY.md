# Privacy Policy — Bath Plan Pro (com.bathplanpro.app)

**Effective Date:** October 2026  
**Application Name:** Bath Plan Pro  
**Package ID:** `com.bathplanpro.app`  
**Developer & Privacy Contact:** rumenborisov500@gmail.com  

## 1. Introduction & Scope
Bath Plan Pro ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Bath Plan Pro Android mobile application (`com.bathplanpro.app`) and web application. Bath Plan Pro is a 2D/3D bathroom layout planner, tile/grout/adhesive quantity calculator, and renovation cost estimator.

## 2. Information We Collect & Store
- **Account & Authentication Information (Firebase Authentication & Cloud Firestore):** When you register or sign in via Email/Password or Google Sign-In, we process your email address, display name, and unique user identifier (`uid`).
- **Bathroom Project Data:** When you save a bathroom renovation project, we store the room dimensions, fixtures, tile parameters, and cost estimates associated with your account in Cloud Firestore and local device storage.
- **Subscriptions (Google Play Billing):** Paid subscriptions (`bathplanpro_pro_monthly` and `bathplanpro_pro_yearly`) are processed directly by Google Play Billing. We never collect or store credit card or bank details.
- **No Advertising ID:** Bath Plan Pro does **not** use the Android Advertising ID and does **not** include third-party advertising SDKs.

## 3. Data Security & Sharing
All data transmitted between the app and Google Firebase / Google Play services is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. We do **not** sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties or advertisers.

## 4. Data Retention & Account Deletion
You can delete your account and all associated data at any time:
1. **Inside the App:** Open **Account & Plan** -> click **Delete Account & Data**.
2. **On the Web / By Email:** Visit our dedicated Account Deletion page at [DELETE_ACCOUNT.md](./DELETE_ACCOUNT.md) or send an email to **rumenborisov500@gmail.com** with the subject `Delete My Bath Plan Pro Account`. All personal and project data is permanently deleted within 7 business days.
