Account & Data Deletion — Bath Plan Pro

Application: Bath Plan Pro (com.bathplanpro.app) · Support & Privacy Contact: privacy@bathplanpro.com

Users of Bath Plan Pro can permanently delete their account and all associated personal and project data at any time using either of the two methods below.

Method 1: Instant Automated Account Deletion (In-App or Web)

  1. Sign in to your Bath Plan Pro account inside the Android app or on the web at /delete-account (or /account).
  2. Scroll to the Data Privacy & Account Deletion section.
  3. Click Delete Account & Data and confirm Yes, Permanently Delete My Account.
Open Interactive Account Deletion Tool

Method 2: Request Deletion via Email (Without Signing In to the App)

If you have already uninstalled the Bath Plan Pro app or cannot access your account, send an email from the address registered with your Bath Plan Pro account to privacy@bathplanpro.com with the subject line Account & Data Deletion Request — Bath Plan Pro. Manual deletion requests are completed within 7 business days.

Send Email Deletion Request

What Data Is Deleted vs. Retained

  • Permanently Deleted Immediately:
    • Your Firebase Authentication account record (uid, email address, login credential).
    • Your Cloud Firestore user profile document (/users/{userId}).
    • All saved 2D bathroom projects, room dimensions, fixture layouts, tile/grout/adhesive calculations, and cost estimates (/projects/{projectId}).
    • All locally cached project and subscription data stored on your device.
  • Active Subscriptions & Statutory Financial Records:
    • Deleting your Bath Plan Pro account does not automatically cancel an active subscription billed through Google Play. Please cancel active subscriptions in the Google Play Store app under Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Bath Plan Pro.
    • Google Play retains transaction receipt records as required by applicable tax and financial compliance laws.
